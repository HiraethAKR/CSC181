## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
  -Visitors & Tourists

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
  -Visitors and tourists alike are usually unable to map out the area of a festival. This might be a bit of fun if you're planning to experience it for the first time, but if an accident were to occur, such as stolen/missing items, medical emergencies, etc., being unable to navigate would lead to a lot of problems.

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
  -They are usually there to enjoy the local specialties and festivities.

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
  -In my own experience, large festivals like diyandi can cause a bit of confusion as to where I should go if I want to do something such as a public restrooms, police stations, medical emergency, and even a specific stall. While this would be a fun event to explore, with such a large amount of people, mapping out the area before I can enjoy myself can be a bit of a hassle.

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  
  -A community driven app where users can see the map of the stalls, police and medical stations (as well as their emergency lines), public restrooms, and other facilities. The app would give a base map of the available data, prioritizing the vital facilities such as police and medical stations, while stalls can be contributed by other users if the data is unavailable.

**How would it help the intended users?**  
  -Visitors and tourists no longer need to worry about navigation and can enjoy the fiesta without worry.
